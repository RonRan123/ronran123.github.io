As a Coding it Forward fellow embedded with the City of Boston, I worked as a product
manager on the city's open data portal — 13,000+ monthly users, most of them arriving
with a specific question and no patience for a taxonomy.

I ran the initial user and event exploration on that traffic and used it to cut the
average number of clicks needed to reach the most-requested datasets. It is a small
intervention, and it is the kind of thing that decides whether public data is actually
public.
