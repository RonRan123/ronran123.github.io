Forge is a student-run software consultancy at UVA. As engineering director I designed
and shipped the MVP of its recruiting platform — matching 750+ students against 120+
companies, and cutting time-to-match by 15%.

Two things I'm prouder of than the product itself. I founded the consulting branch that
took on short- and medium-term projects around the University community, which grew
engineering revenue 22%. And I taught 71 students software engineering through a
140-hour course built to get them ready for a nine-week internship — which turned out
to be the most durable thing I built there.
